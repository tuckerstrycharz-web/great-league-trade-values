        How the numbers work

        Format

        • Everything is for this league: 12 teams, full PPR, 1QB
        • Charts that don't match are left out (FantasyPros: scoring unstated; Underdog: no redraft values)

        Consensus value (default)

        • Each source ranks players: CBS, RSJ, FantasyCalc, ESPN points above replacement
        • Each rank is read off one shared curve (average of the four, #1 = 100), then averaged
        • Ranking first stops one source's steep or flat scale from dominating
        • Sources agree closely: rank correlation 0.88–0.94 on the top 150
        • Earlier weeks move by the average change of sources covering that week
        • Weeks 1–4 use CBS + ESPN only; RSJ and FantasyCalc enter from Week 4

        CBS (weekly history)

        • Dave Richard's full-PPR column; QBs on the 1QB 4-pt TD scale
        • Every week since Week 1 (Sep 7) on a steady scale, ~125 players
        • Off chart = worth 4 or less · not used alone in the trade builder (no league size stated)

        RSJ and FantasyCalc

        • RSJ: built for 12-team, full-PPR, 1QB; only Week 4 (Sep 29) and Week 8 saved
        • RSJ Week 4 restated to the Week 8 scale by overall rank
        • FantasyCalc: real redraft trades in 12-team PPR 1QB leagues; board shows its 30-day change

        ESPN

        • Rest-of-season projections in this league's own scoring
        • Value = projected points minus a replacement line at his position
        • Replacement = halfway between the last starter and the waiver line · at or below it = 0

        Trade builder

        • All values out of 100 (each source's #1 = 100)
        • Each side's total = value received + best-player bonus (WalterPicks rule)
        • Rule fit to four WalterPicks results from this league (e.g. CMC for Nacua: +3.3)
        • Fair = within 5%
